Pocket mode

For a phone in a pocket or an armband. During the run the screen goes black (black pixels on the iPhone screen use almost no power) and the map stops drawing, but GPS and the voice keep going. Tap the screen to look for 12 s; when it goes black again, the run settings close. Also in the run settings (hold the gear 5 s).

Do not lock the phone with the side button: iOS freezes web apps while the phone is locked, so there is no GPS and no voice until you unlock it. The app then catches up and says the gap.

Live mode gun time
8:00:00

LIVE starts the clock at this time, then switches to your chip time as you cross the start line, so a gun a few seconds early or late does not matter. Change it only if the start is delayed by minutes.

Before you start

Two things to turn on. Tap a button, then choose Allow when the iPhone asks.

1. LocationNeeded. Puts you on the course and times you against the ghost.
2. CompassOptional. Turns the map the way you face, before the run. The iPhone calls it “motion and orientation”.

Later on: the buttons at the top of the map, or Help → Location.
